Più forte la luce, più profonda l'ombra

Alabaster & bronze  |  2025
H 64  ·  D 25  ·  W 40 cm

I want to speak of that light which sometimes, unexpectedly, passes beside me.
It captures me and makes my eyes shine.
It always flees from me — and I, in love, heart in my throat,
parched, consumed, seek it everywhere.
