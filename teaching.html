Lorenzo Galligani

Teaching & Sculpture

Sculptor and sculpture educator based in Florence, Italy. My teaching combines traditional sculptural techniques, direct experience with materials, and the development of each student's individual artistic language.


Teaching Philosophy

Art is a fundamental language in every student's educational journey: a powerful tool for inner exploration and connection with the outside world.

From the Greek wisdom of “Know thyself” to the Enlightenment motto “Dare to know”, my educational aim is to guide students in using artistic expression as a means of critical, creative and personal understanding.

Teaching & Technical Skills

Planning and development of individual and group sculpture courses; educational programmes for international students and different levels of experience; practical demonstrations and mentoring; step-by-step visual guidance in traditional techniques; support in the development of critical thinking, spatial composition and individual artistic language.

Techniques include marble and stone carving, clay and plaster modelling, mould making, resins, wood carving, restoration and conservation of stone and wood, artistic lost-wax casting, metalwork and patination.
